# Third-party components

- **Inter font**, copyright the Inter Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Bundled font and its license are in `web/assets/`.
- ComfyUI is a separately installed dependency. This package calls its loaders, samplers and LoRA implementation; it does not bundle ComfyUI or model weights.
- **ComfyUI-Krea2T-Enhancer** is an optional, separately installed dependency for Krea enhancement. Its upstream license applies: https://github.com/capitan01R/ComfyUI-Krea2T-Enhancer. No enhancer source is bundled.
- NumPy, Pillow and aiohttp are separately installed dependencies under their respective licenses.

The MIT license covers DMAI NODES source. Model weights, LoRAs and optional dependencies retain their own terms. No private character LoRAs, provider keys or sample prompts from client workflows are distributed.
